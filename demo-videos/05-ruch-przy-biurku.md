## Post-production

- **Hook (0–3 s):** „Nie masz godziny? Masz minutę.” — *No hour to spare? You have a minute.*
- **Closing card (last 2–3 s):** „GYM TRACKER — każdy ruch się liczy” — *GYM TRACKER — every move counts*
- **Music:** Playful, bouncy pop around 120 BPM; celebratory sting on each badge.
- **Length:** keep the edit between 25 and 45 s. Cut inside a beat, never across a tap, so every action keeps its cause and its result.
- **Style:** see the shared guide in [README.md](README.md#post-production-guide).
