# 08 — Cutting: calories and protein under control

*Redukcja: kalorie i białko pod kontrolą*

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Need** | „Jestem na redukcji — muszę trafić w kalorie i białko” — I'm cutting — I need to hit my calories and protein |
| **Persona** | Lifter on a cut, 3 kg down, who must keep protein high to keep the muscle. |
| **Problem** | Counting calories is tedious, and a plain counter does not say what to eat next to close the protein gap — nor whether the weight trend is on track. |

## Post-production

- **Hook (0–3 s):** „Brakuje 107 g białka. Co teraz?” — *107 g of protein to go. What now?*
- **Closing card (last 2–3 s):** „GYM TRACKER — dieta, która się dopasowuje” — *GYM TRACKER — a diet that adapts*
- **Music:** Clean, positive electro-pop around 108 BPM.
- **Length:** keep the edit between 25 and 45 s. Cut inside a beat, never across a tap, so every action keeps its cause and its result.
- **Style:** see the shared guide in [README.md](README.md#post-production-guide).
