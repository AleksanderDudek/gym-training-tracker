## Post-production

- **Hook (0–3 s):** „Zero sprzętu. Zero wymówek.” — *No equipment. No excuses.*
- **Closing card (last 2–3 s):** „GYM TRACKER — plan, który mówi, co dziś” — *GYM TRACKER — a plan that tells you what’s today*
- **Music:** Warm, upbeat acoustic-pop around 100 BPM; light and encouraging.
- **Length:** keep the edit between 25 and 45 s. Cut inside a beat, never across a tap, so every action keeps its cause and its result.
- **Style:** see the shared guide in [README.md](README.md#post-production-guide).
