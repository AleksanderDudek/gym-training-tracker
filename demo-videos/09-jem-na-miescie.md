# 09 — Eating out without wrecking the diet

*Jem na mieście — i trzymam dietę*

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Need** | „Jem na mieście, np. w KFC” — I eat out — at KFC, for example |
| **Persona** | Busy person who eats out with friends or at work and does not want to give up on the plan. |
| **Problem** | Fast food menus hide their numbers in PDFs; guessing a meal’s calories and protein usually means guessing wrong. |
| **How the app helps** | Menus of 18 chains with numbers from their own nutrition tables (source and date shown), sorted by most protein, each item marked against your diet — „pasuje”, „może być”, „nie pasuje” — and logged to the diary in one tap. |
