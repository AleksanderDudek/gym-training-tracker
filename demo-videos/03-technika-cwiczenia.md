# 03 — How to do the exercise right

*Jak dobrze zrobić ćwiczenie*

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Need** | „Nie wiem, jak dobrze zrobić to ćwiczenie” — I'm not sure I'm doing the exercise right |
| **Persona** | Beginner afraid of doing a move wrong — or of looking silly at the gym. |
| **Problem** | Form advice is scattered over long videos full of ads; a beginner needs the two cues that matter and to see the movement, now. |

## Post-production

- **Hook (0–3 s):** „Robisz pompki dobrze?” — *Are your push-ups right?*
- **Closing card (last 2–3 s):** „GYM TRACKER — 114 ćwiczeń z techniką” — *GYM TRACKER — 114 exercises with form cues*
- **Music:** Calm, focused lo-fi around 85 BPM; soft hit when the figure is paused.
- **Length:** keep the edit between 25 and 45 s. Cut inside a beat, never across a tap, so every action keeps its cause and its result.
- **Style:** see the shared guide in [README.md](README.md#post-production-guide).
