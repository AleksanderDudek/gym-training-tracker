# 07 — A plan that keeps you on schedule

*Plan, który pilnuje terminów*

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Need** | „Zawsze rzucam po dwóch tygodniach” — I always quit after two weeks |
| **Persona** | On-and-off trainee: starts strong every January, gone by February. |
| **Problem** | Without dates, a plan is a wish. One missed day turns into a missed week, and the streak — and the habit — is gone. |
| **How the app helps** | The plan puts every session on a real date and keeps score: points, a rank, a streak, a journal of every session. A missed day is not a failure — the session waits and can be made up, marked „nadrobiony”. |
| **Key message** | Plan pilnuje terminów — i wybacza jeden gorszy dzień. — *The plan keeps the dates — and forgives a bad day.* |
| **Screens shown** | Dziś (today’s session) · Plan: weeks, points, rank, made-up sessions, journal |

- **Hook (0–3 s):** „Tydzień 7. Zero opuszczonych.” — *Week 7. Nothing missed.*
- **Closing card (last 2–3 s):** „GYM TRACKER — regularność bez spiny” — *GYM TRACKER — consistency without stress*
- **Music:** Steady, motivating indie-electronic around 112 BPM; a lift when the week list expands.
- **Length:** keep the edit between 25 and 45 s. Cut inside a beat, never across a tap, so every action keeps its cause and its result.
